# Privacy policy

*Last updated: September 2026*

Auspicia does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information.

## What stays on your device

The app has no servers and makes no network connections of its own. Your birth date and time, your home's layout, and your preferences are saved only on your iPhone, and they are used only to produce readings inside the app. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

The home screen widget reads the same information from your device. It does not send it anywhere either.

## iCloud

If you use iCloud, your settings may sync between your own devices through your Apple Account. That sync is handled entirely by Apple. We have no access to it.

## Purchases

Auspicia Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment, and we never see your payment details.

## Analytics and tracking

None. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

## If you email us

If you write to support@auspicia.app, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you, and we don't add you to any mailing list. You can ask us to delete your correspondence at any time.

## This website

auspicia.app is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics, or third-party scripts, fonts, or images. Like any web host, GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security and reliability; see [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Children

Auspicia is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: [support@auspicia.app](mailto:support@auspicia.app)
