Three lenses, one honest answer

Most apps give you one opinion. Auspicia checks three independent traditions and shows each one side by side, so you can see where they agree and where they don't.

曆

Almanac Day

The traditional Tong Shu reading for the date: its day officer, stem and branch, and the activities it favors and warns against.

命

Your BaZi

How the day's pillar interacts with your own birth chart. The same date can be excellent for one person and a clash for another.

宅

Feng Shui

Flying Star influences for your home's facing direction, including which sectors are active this month and what to do about them.

吉

When all three lenses rate a day favorable, it earns the gold 吉 mark. Those are the days worth circling. Everything else gets a clear rating from Very Auspicious to Avoid, with the reasons one tap away.
