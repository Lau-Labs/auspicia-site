# Help and support

## Getting in touch

For questions, problems, or feedback, email [support@auspicia.app](mailto:support@auspicia.app). It helps to include your iPhone model and iOS version.

## Common questions

**Why don't the lenses always agree?**
They're three different traditions looking at the same day. When all three agree, the day gets a gold 吉. Most days they don't, and that's normal.

**Do I need to know my birth time?**
No. An approximate time is fine, and the app works without one.

**How do I restore my purchase on a new device?**
Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. You'll need to be signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

**Where is my data stored?**
Only on your iPhone. See the [privacy policy](privacy) for details.

**Is this advice?**
No. Auspicia is for entertainment and cultural interest purposes only. It provides no advice of any kind, whether medical, financial, legal, or otherwise.
